Petty Cash Advance Liquidation

Overview

Petty Cash Advances shall be liquidated immediately after the purpose for which the cash advance was granted has been accomplished. Liquidation documents must be complete, properly supported, and submitted within the prescribed period.

Documentary Requirements

RequirementNumber of Copies
Accomplished Petty Cash Voucher (PCV)2 Sets (1 Original, 1 CTC/Photocopy)
Original Official Receipts / Sales Invoices and supporting documentsOriginal + 1 CTC/Photocopy
Refund of any unutilized cash advance, if applicableAs Applicable
Other supporting documents required by the Cashier SectionAs Required

Procedure

  1. Accomplish the Petty Cash Voucher (PCV) completely and accurately.
  2. Attach all original receipts and supporting documents.
  3. Refund any unutilized portion of the Petty Cash Advance, if applicable.
  4. Leave all date fields intended for the Petty Cash Custodian blank.
  5. Submit two (2) complete sets of liquidation documents to the Cashier Section.
  6. Await confirmation of acceptance and processing of the liquidation.

Important Reminders

  • Liquidate the Petty Cash Advance within five (5) working days from the date of availment.
  • Failure to liquidate within the prescribed period may result in salary deduction in accordance with existing rules and regulations.
  • Under "Payee/Office", indicate the proper office or committee.
  • The "Particulars" field shall contain a complete description of the expense incurred.
  • Do not indicate the date in the PCV. Date fields intended for the Petty Cash Custodian shall be left blank.
  • Do not place adhesive tape over thermal receipts.
  • Attach only original receipts and supporting documents.
  • Submit two (2) complete sets (1 Original and 1 CTC/Photocopy), properly sorted.
  • Use the latest approved Petty Cash Voucher template.
  • Incomplete documentary requirements may delay processing.
Reminder: Ensure all receipts are readable and legible before submission. Faded or damaged thermal receipts may not be accepted without adequate supporting documentation.
