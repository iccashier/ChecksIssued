Petty Cash Advance Liquidation

Overview

Petty Cash Advance Liquidation refers to the submission of documentary requirements and supporting documents to account for a previously granted Petty Cash Advance.

Requestors are required to liquidate their Petty Cash Advance within five (5) working days from the date of availment in accordance with existing policies and guidelines.

Procedure

  1. Accomplish the Petty Cash Voucher (PCV) completely and accurately.
  2. Prepare and attach the documentary requirements based on the nature of the expense incurred.
  3. Attach all original receipts and supporting documents.
  4. Refund any unused portion of the Petty Cash Advance to the Cashier Section, if applicable.
  5. Leave all date fields intended for the Petty Cash Custodian blank.
  6. Submit the complete liquidation documents to the Cashier Section.
  7. Await confirmation of the acceptance and processing of the liquidation.

Important Reminders

  • The requestor shall liquidate the Petty Cash Advance within five (5) working days from the date of availment.
  • Failure to liquidate the Petty Cash Advance within the prescribed period shall result in the automatic deduction of the full amount from the requestor's salary without need of notice.
  • Under "Payee/Office", indicate the office or committee to which the budget shall be charged.
  • The "Particulars" field shall contain a complete description of the nature of the expense incurred.
  • Do not indicate the date in the Petty Cash Voucher (PCV). Date fields designated for the Petty Cash Fund (PCF) Custodian shall be left blank.
  • The approver shall be the Immediate Supervisor of the requestor in accordance with Office Order No. 2023-148, as amended.
  • Prepare and submit two (2) complete sets of documentary requirements (1 Original and 1 Certified True Copy (CTC)), properly sorted.
  • Attach all original receipts and supporting documents, as applicable.
  • Do not place adhesive tape on thermal receipts, as it may cause the printed details to fade or become unreadable over time.
  • Other documentary requirements and supporting attachments may be required depending on the nature of the transaction.
  • Incomplete documentary requirements may result in delays in processing.
  • Petty Cash Advances shall be utilized only for their approved purpose.
  • Requests remain subject to existing Commission policies, accounting rules, auditing regulations, and availability of funds.
