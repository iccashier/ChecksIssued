Petty Cash Advance Request

Overview

Petty Cash Advances may be requested for authorized representation, transportation, and other miscellaneous expenses, subject to existing policies, guidelines, and the availability of funds.

Employees are advised to ensure that all documentary requirements are complete prior to submission to facilitate timely processing.

Documentary Requirements

Category Requirement Number of Copies
Representation (Meals) Approved Petty Cash Voucher (PCV) 2 Original Copies
Purchase Request 2 Original Copies
Notice of Meeting 2 Copies (1 Original, 1 Certified True Copy)
Representation (Groceries) Approved Petty Cash Voucher (PCV) 2 Original Copies
Purchase Request 2 Original Copies
Transportation Approved Petty Cash Voucher (PCV) 2 Original Copies

Locator Slip (if applicable) 2 Copies (1 Original, 1 Certified True Copy)

Other documentary requirements and supporting attachments as may be deemed necessary As Required

Procedure

  1. Accomplish the Petty Cash Voucher (PCV) completely and accurately.
  2. Obtain the approval of the Immediate Supervisor or higher approving authority, as applicable.
  3. Prepare and attach the documentary requirements based on the nature of the request.
  4. Submit the complete documentary requirements to the Cashier Section.
  5. Await notification regarding the release of the approved petty cash advance.

Important Reminders

  • Only plantilla personnel are authorized to avail of Petty Cash Advances.
  • Splitting of transactions is not allowed.
  • Under "Payee/Office", indicate the office or committee to which the budget shall be charged.
  • The "Particulars" field shall contain a complete description of the nature of the disbursement or expense.
  • Do not indicate the date in the Petty Cash Voucher (PCV). Date fields shall be filled out by the Petty Cash Fund (PCF) Custodian.
  • Indicate the amount in the "Total Amount Granted" field.
  • The approver shall be the Immediate Supervisor of the requestor in accordance with Office Order No. 2023-148, as amended.
  • Prepare and submit two (2) complete sets of documentary requirements (1 Original and 1 Certified True Copy (CTC)), properly sorted.
  • Petty Cash Advances shall be utilized only for their approved purpose.
  • Do not place adhesive tape on thermal receipts, as it may cause the printed details to fade or become unreadable over time.
  • Other documentary requirements and supporting attachments may be required depending on the nature of the transaction.
  • Incomplete documentary requirements may result in delays in processing.
  • Requests remain subject to existing Commission policies, accounting rules, auditing regulations, and availability of funds.
