Petty Cash Advance Request

Overview

Petty Cash Advances may be requested for authorized miscellaneous and transportation expenses, subject to existing Commission policies and availability of funds. Ensure documentary requirements are complete before submission to facilitate timely processing.

Procedure

  1. Accomplish the Petty Cash Voucher (PCV) completely and accurately.
  2. Obtain approval from the Immediate Supervisor or higher approving authority.
  3. Attach the required supporting documents.
  4. Submit two (2) complete sets to the Cashier Section.
  5. Await notification regarding release of the approved Petty Cash Advance.

Important Reminders

  • Indicate the proper Office/Committee under Payee/Office.
  • Provide a complete description under Particulars.
  • Leave all Petty Cash Custodian date fields blank.
  • For Petty Cash Advances, indicate the amount only in Total Amount Granted.
  • The approver shall be the Immediate Supervisor pursuant to Office Order No. 2023-148, as amended.
  • Submit two (2) complete sets (1 Original and 1 CTC/Photocopy), properly sorted.
  • Only plantilla personnel are authorized to avail of Petty Cash Advances.
  • Splitting of transactions is not allowed.
  • Do not place adhesive tape over thermal receipts.
  • Use the PCV template in Downloadable Forms.
  • Incomplete documentary requirements may delay processing.
Note: Requests remain subject to Commission policies, COA rules, auditing regulations, and availability of funds.
