Petty Cash Guide

Petty Cash Reimbursement

Minimum Documentary Requirements:

  • Approved Petty Cash Voucher (PCV)
  • Original Official Receipt
  • Supporting Documents, if applicable

Petty Cash Advance

Minimum Documentary Requirements:

  • Approved Request for Petty Cash Advance
  • Supporting Documents
  • Other documents as may be required

Liquidation of Petty Cash Advance

Minimum Documentary Requirements:

  • Liquidation Report
  • Original Official Receipts
  • Supporting Documents
  • Refund of Excess Cash Advance, if any
